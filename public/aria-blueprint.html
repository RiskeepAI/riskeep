Qué es ARIA

Qué hace, cómo decide y por qué está pensada para no arriesgar de más

01 / 06

ARIA (Autonomous Risk Intelligence Agent) es un agente de trading que opera en Bitget Futures (contratos perpetuos USDT-M). Analiza el mercado en tiempo real, decide si abrir una posición LONG, SHORT o esperar (HOLD), gestiona el riesgo de cada operación y aprende de cada trade cerrado — todo con las claves de tu propia cuenta de Bitget, que nunca salen de tu ordenador.

⚙️ Cómo decide, en 5 pasos
1

Analiza — indicadores técnicos (RSI, medias móviles, patrones de velas, volumen) en varios marcos temporales a la vez, para varios pares simultáneamente.

2

Decide — un modelo de IA (el que elijas: Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Claude, o uno local sin depender de ningún servicio externo) traduce ese análisis en una decisión con un nivel de confianza.

3

Filtra y valida — un sistema de reglas fijas revisa la decisión antes de operar: descarta entradas con mala relación riesgo:recompensa (contando también las comisiones) y exige liquidez real justo en el momento de ejecutar, no solo cuando se decidió.

4

Ejecuta con protección — coloca la orden intentando la comisión más barata, fija stop loss y take profit automáticos según la volatilidad real del momento, y añade una red de seguridad del propio exchange por si el bot se cayera.

5

Vigila y aprende — revisa las posiciones abiertas constantemente (protege beneficio, cierra si no llega a su objetivo a tiempo), y cada operación cerrada queda guardada como referencia para análisis futuros similares.

⚠️

Una nota honesta

ARIA no promete rentabilidad ni predice el mercado. Su valor es operar con disciplina, gestionar el riesgo de cada operación y minimizar los costes de operar (las comisiones pesan más de lo que la mayoría cree). Rendimientos pasados no garantizan resultados futuros.

Guía paso a paso — crear la API en Bitget Simulated Trading

Crear cuenta en Bitget — regístrate en bitget.com. Es gratuita y no requiere verificación de identidad para el modo simulado.

7

Pégalas en el asistente de ARIA — pestaña «Demo» del paso de APIs. Puedes cambiarlas más adelante desde Configuración en el dashboard sin reinstalar nada.

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • i

  • ✗

Con permisos de solo Futures (sin Withdrawals), la clave del bot no puede retirar, transferir ni acceder a otras cuentas. Ante una clave comprometida, el peor escenario es perder las posiciones abiertas — nunca un vaciado de cuenta.

🎮 Niveles de autonomía — cuánto decide ARIA por sí sola
OBSERVAR

Solo mira — ARIA analiza el mercado y muestra qué haría, pero no abre ninguna posición. Ideal para el primer día, para comprobar que todo funciona antes de arriesgar nada, ni siquiera fondos demo.

MANUAL

Tú apruebas — ARIA propone la operación (símbolo, dirección, tamaño, stop) y espera tu confirmación desde el dashboard o Telegram antes de ejecutarla.

AUTOMÁTICO

Opera sola — ARIA ejecuta sin pedirte confirmación, siempre dentro de los límites de riesgo configurados. Cambia de nivel en cualquier momento desde el dashboard o con /observe /manual /auto en Telegram.

💰 Paper vs. Live — con qué dinero opera

📊 Paper (recomendado para empezar)

Opera contra Bitget Simulated Trading, con fondos virtuales. Cero riesgo real — perfecto para ver cómo se comporta ARIA con tu configuración antes de arriesgar dinero de verdad.

📈 Live

Opera con dinero real en tu cuenta de Bitget. Requiere tu suscripción activa y las claves de tu cuenta real (ver pág. 5). Solo pásate a Live cuando hayas visto suficientes ciclos en Paper con tu configuración.

💡

Progresión recomendada

Observar (Paper) → Manual (Paper) → Automático (Paper), un tiempo prudente en cada nivel → luego, si quieres, el mismo camino en Live empezando también por Manual. Todos los límites de riesgo (cuánto arriesga por operación, cuántas posiciones a la vez, etc.) se ajustan desde Configuración en el dashboard, en cualquier momento.

What is ARIA

What it does, how it decides, and why it's designed not to overreach

01 / 06

ARIA (Autonomous Risk Intelligence Agent) is a trading agent that operates on Bitget Futures (USDT-M perpetual contracts). It analyzes the market in real time, decides whether to open a LONG, SHORT, or wait (HOLD) position, manages the risk of every trade, and learns from every closed trade — all using your own Bitget account's keys, which never leave your computer.

⚙️ How it decides, in 5 steps
1

Analyzes — technical indicators (RSI, moving averages, candlestick patterns, volume) across several timeframes at once, for several pairs simultaneously.

2

Decides — an AI model (your choice: Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Claude, or a local one with no dependency on any external service) turns that analysis into a decision with a confidence level.

3

Filters and validates — a set of fixed rules reviews the decision before trading: it discards entries with a poor risk:reward ratio (fees included) and requires real liquidity right at execution time, not only when the decision was made.

4

Executes with protection — places the order trying for the cheapest fee, sets automatic stop loss and take profit based on real-time volatility, and adds a safety net from the exchange itself in case the bot goes down.

5

Watches and learns — constantly reviews open positions (locks in profit, closes if it doesn't reach its target in time), and every closed trade is kept as reference for similar future analysis.

⚠️

An honest note

ARIA doesn't promise returns or predict the market. Its value is trading with discipline, managing the risk of every trade, and minimizing the cost of trading (fees weigh more than most people think). Past performance doesn't guarantee future results.

Simulated Trading account, required permissions, and the 3 authentication credentials

📋

Step-by-step guide — creating the API on Bitget Simulated Trading

1

Create a Bitget account — sign up at bitget.com. It's free and doesn't require identity verification for simulated mode.

2

Turn on Simulated Trading — go to Trade → Futures and enable «Simulated Trading» from the interface menu.

3

Create the API from the simulator — once inside Simulated Trading, go to your profile → API Management → Create API Key. Create it from there, not from your main account.

4

Permissions to enable — Futures with Read-Write access. You don't need to enable Withdrawals — ARIA never needs it.

5

Save the 3 credentials — Bitget gives you: API Key, Secret Key and Passphrase (you choose it when creating the key). Copy them now — the Passphrase can't be recovered later.

6

Get demo funds — inside the simulator, go to Assets → Transfer and move USDT from your Spot balance to your USDT-M Futures balance. It's virtual money, no real cost.

7

Paste them into the ARIA wizard — the «Demo» tab in the APIs step. You can change them later from Settings in the dashboard without reinstalling anything.

✅

THE 3 CREDENTIALS

  • ✓
    API Key — public identifier of the key
  • ✓
    Secret Key — private key used to sign requests
  • ✓
    Passphrase — the extra password you set when creating the API
  • i
    All 3 are required for Demo mode. For Live, repeat the process from your real Bitget account.
🚫

NEVER DO THIS

  • ✗
    Enable Withdrawals — ARIA never needs to withdraw funds
  • ✗
    Create the key from your main account — for Demo, the key must come from the simulator
  • ✗
    Use the demo key in Live mode — they're different environments, always keep them separate
  • ✗
    Share your keys — not with Riskeep, not with anyone. They only ever live on your machine.
🔒

Principle of least privilege

With Futures-only permissions (no Withdrawals), the bot's key can't withdraw, transfer, or access other accounts. Even if a key were compromised, the worst case is losing open positions — never an emptied account.

🎮 Autonomy levels — how much ARIA decides on its own
OBSERVE

Just watches — ARIA analyzes the market and shows what it would do, but doesn't open any position. Ideal for day one, to confirm everything works before risking anything, not even demo funds.

MANUAL

You approve — ARIA proposes the trade (symbol, direction, size, stop) and waits for your confirmation from the dashboard or Telegram before executing it.

AUTOMATIC

Trades on its own — ARIA executes without asking you to confirm, always within the configured risk limits. Switch levels at any time from the dashboard, or with /observe /manual /auto on Telegram.

💰 Paper vs. Live — whose money it trades with

📊 Paper (recommended to start)

Trades against Bitget Simulated Trading, with virtual funds. Zero real risk — perfect for seeing how ARIA behaves with your configuration before risking real money.

📈 Live

Trades with real money in your Bitget account. Requires your active subscription and your real account's keys (see p. 5). Only switch to Live once you've seen enough cycles in Paper with your configuration.

💡

Recommended progression

Observe (Paper) → Manual (Paper) → Automatic (Paper), a sensible amount of time at each level → then, if you want, the same path in Live starting with Manual too. All risk limits (how much it risks per trade, how many positions at once, etc.) are adjusted from Settings in the dashboard, at any time.
